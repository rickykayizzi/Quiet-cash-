Kuza Plan

An endowment plan — save toward a future goal while your beneficiaries are protected if you pass away or become disabled early.

Ask Richard About This Plan Take the Insurance Finder

What it covers

  • Investment account with maturity payout
  • Death benefit
  • Permanent & total disability cover
  • Critical illness cover (optional)
  • Physical impairment cover (optional)
  • Can be used as loan collateral after 3 years
Eligibility & terms: Ages 18–60. Fixed 10-year term. Sum assured tiers: 20M, 40M, 60M, 80M, or 100M UGX. No medical tests required. Maximum one Kuza Plan policy per person.
